**Status**: Published in the IUI 2026 Companion Proceedings (Demos Track)

EvalAgent supports comparative evaluation of computer-using GUI agents across models, personas, and user-defined criteria. It visualizes full execution trajectories and highlights the trace segments most relevant to each evaluation result.

    Ph.D. Student in Computer Science at Purdue University

    I study how people and AI systems interact, how language models reason, and how we can evaluate intelligent agents in ways that reflect human values.

    Advised by Prof. Jason Wu.

    View Publications Email Me

    I received my B.S. in Computer Science, cum laude, from the University of Notre Dame in May 2026. My earlier research with SaNDwich Lab, MINE Lab, and the FORESEER Group spanned web-agent evaluation, value alignment, reasoning calibration, and benchmark analysis.

    Research

    Research interests

    Human–AI Interaction

    Designing systems that help people collaborate with AI agents in useful, usable, and human-centered ways.

    Agent Evaluation

    Developing rigorous methods that capture interaction quality, behavioral alignment, and full execution processes beyond task success.

    LLM Reasoning & Interpretability

    Understanding, probing, and calibrating how language models reason and make decisions in real-world systems.
